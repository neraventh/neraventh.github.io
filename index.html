NERAVENTH

The AI Game Master

“The Keeper of Possible Paths”

The Game Master creates the possibilities.
The players create the story.

Neraventh is a Game Master for fantasy tabletop adventures, compatible with the fifth edition rules. You tell it what your hero does. It tells the story out loud, gives a voice to the people you meet, and rolls the dice on a real 3D table.

  • Open source and free. No account, no subscription.
  • Runs on your PC. Your saves stay at home. Use a cloud AI or one that runs on your own machine.
  • Fair dice. The rules and every roll are handled by code. The AI tells the story, but it never makes up a number.
  • Your choices. The Game Master never decides for you. Your hero is yours.
  • A real table. A 3D diorama with miniatures, torchlight and dice you can watch roll.
